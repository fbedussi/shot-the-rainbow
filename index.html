How to play

You have to create a 🌈 pedestrian cross to help the 🦄 to cross the road.

You will see a color, you need to find something around you with the same color, frame it with the camera and tap on it.

The closer you get, the more points you'll earn.

If it's not close enought the color won't be accepted.
