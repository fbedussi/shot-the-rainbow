How to play

This is a tale of two 🦄, John and Jean.

Jean is waiting for John on the other side of the road. She is very impatient and will go away in a short time if John doesn't reach her quickly.

But John cannot cross the road, since there is no pedestrian crossing.

Unicorns can only use special 🌈 pedestrian crossing, help him in creating one!

For every crossing stripe, you will see a color. You need to find something around you with the same color, frame it with the camera and tap on it.

The closer you get, the more points you'll earn.

If it's not close enough the color won't be accepted.

Good luck!
